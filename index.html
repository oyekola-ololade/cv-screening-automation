  • Candidate fields and supplied text are normalized.
  • Google Sheets append/update nodes exist.
  • Role requirements are provided to an AI evaluation step.
  • Structured output parsing and score clamping exist.
  • Explicit success and failure response nodes exist.

What it does not prove

  • Reliable end-to-end execution in the current export.
  • Real duplicate detection.
  • Binary PDF/image CV parsing.
  • Production-safe candidate-data handling.
  • Autonomous hiring decisions or production compliance.
